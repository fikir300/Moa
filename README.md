Team MOA Engineering Notebook

Overview

This engineering notebook documents the journey of Team MOA, a robotics team from Addis Ababa Science and Technology University, created for the VEX V5 Push Back Competition. It covers the complete process of designing, building, programming, testing, and improving the team’s robot.



Project Goals

Build a competitive and reliable robot

Gain practical engineering experience

Improve teamwork and problem-solving skills

Develop programming and mechanical design abilities



Robot Design

The robot was designed with:

Gear and chain mechanisms

Reinforced structural supports

Intake and scoring systems

Efficient movement and control systems


The strategy focused on:

Scoring consistently

Fast movement

Reliable parking during endgame



Development Process

The project included:

1. Designing the robot structure


2. Building the base and gear systems


3. Installing chains and intake mechanisms


4. Programming movement and controls


5. Testing and improving performance


6. Reinforcing weak structural areas



Testing & Improvements

Multiple tests were conducted to improve:

Robot movement

Scoring accuracy

Gear alignment

Intake performance

Driver control


Challenges such as gear misalignment, instability, and programming issues were solved through redesigning, reinforcement, and repeated testing.


Future Plans

Future improvements include:

Faster intake systems

Better automation and sensors

Improved structural efficiency

Advanced programming features


Conclusion

This project helped Team MOA gain valuable experience in robotics, engineering design, programming, and teamwork. The experience strengthened the team’s technical and problem-solving skills while preparing them for future robotics competitions.
